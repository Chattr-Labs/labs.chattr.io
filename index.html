01 / ABOUT

One studio, many experiments.

Chattr Labs is the home for software projects while they're being built, tested, and figured out.

Some projects will grow into products of their own. Some will stay small and useful. Some will be fun for a while and then retire gracefully. Chattr Labs gives each of them a stable place to live in the meantime — one company behind the apps, the domains, and the support inbox.

There's no grand theme. The common thread is simply care: well-made software, built by hand, for problems worth solving.

HOW IT WORKS
  • 01 Start with a real problem, usually our own
  • 02 Build it carefully and use it daily
  • 03 Share it once it's genuinely ready
  • 04 Let the best ones grow up and move out
